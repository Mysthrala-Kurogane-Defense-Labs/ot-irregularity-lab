## Status and limits

This is a locally generated, integrity-checked release candidate. It has not been uploaded as a GitHub release. Its event and process-profile distributions are synthetic suite outcomes, not estimates of industrial prevalence. The centrifugal VFD profile remains simplified and is not a pump-specific digital twin.
