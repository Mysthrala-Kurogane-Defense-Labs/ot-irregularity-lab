# Public dataset pattern review

This review records how existing datasets inform scenario coverage. It does not copy third-party samples or treat one dataset's event frequency as industrial prevalence. All released OT Irregularity datasets remain synthetic and non-customer.

## Scenario decisions

- `air_leak` targets compressor assets and affects only `pressure_bar` and `motor_current_a`. Pressure decreases progressively; current increases as a simplified compensation response. Its numeric fractions and difficulty scaling are scenario parameters, not fitted MetroPT estimates.
- The training and challenge v0.3/v0.2 profiles add compressor-containing cells while retaining prior single-asset and mixed profiles. This is a benchmark coverage choice to make the compressor-specific event sampleable more often, not a claim about compressor prevalence in factories.
- The first 300-run seeded validation sample contained 12 air leaks, all in train. After increasing the explicit suite sampling weight, the next 300-run sample contained 26 (21 train, 4 validation, 1 test); a separate 3,000-run sizing draw contained 272 (196 train, 32 validation, 44 test). These are draw-specific counts, not field prevalence or statistical power claims. Manifests expose realized per-partition coverage.
- Bearing degradation remains a slow scalar process effect. Available bearing sources include high-frequency signals and controlled rigs; they do not validate the present low-rate telemetry magnitudes.
- Event types, severity distributions and suite weights remain explicit benchmark choices. MetroPT's small set of maintenance events is insufficient to estimate failure prevalence.
- Normal-operation and false-positive-stress suites remain separate from injected fault labels. Data reported as cleaned/normal-only can inform normal-scenario diversity, but not anomaly priors.
- Public datasets and detector repositories are research references only. The generation runtime, challenge evaluator and benchmark do not import or execute external models.

## Reproducible release

The new `suites/training-v0.3.yaml` and `suites/challenge-v0.2.yaml` add an independent, seeded distribution for `air_leak`; prior suite versions are retained unchanged. Compressor-containing asset profiles and a higher explicit event weight increase its chance of being sampleable while retaining other asset mixes. Generate with the repository's pinned environment, record the suite and simulator versions, inspect realized event/asset distributions, and publish only synthetic Parquet plus its independent labels artifact. See [dataset generation](DATASETS.md) and [scenario definitions](SCENARIOS.md).

## Sources

- UCI's MetroPT-3 record documents the sensors, maintenance-report labels, data DOI and CC BY 4.0 license. Its metadata contains differing nominal sampling descriptions; the project's calibration report measures timestamp cadence from the downloaded file rather than treating either description as a perfect grid.
- The peer-reviewed MetroPT dataset paper describes the air-leak pressure and compensation cases: [Almeida et al., Scientific Data 9, 764 (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9747912/).
- MIMII's official [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/3384388) states its machine types, fault recordings, SNR variants and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.
- The University of Paderborn [Bearing DataCenter](https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/en/kat/research/bearing-datacenter) states its signals, operating-condition coverage and CC BY-NC 4.0 terms.
- iTrust/SUTD's [CI-AI Challenge dataset page](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/itrust/ci-ai-challenge-2024/resources/data/) distinguishes selected normal-only releases from attack data and notes the normal data was cleaned.
- DTU's [extended TEP dataset record](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/an-extended-tennessee-eastman-simulation-dataset-for-fault-detect/) describes repeated simulations, multiple disturbance magnitudes and operating-mode transitions.
